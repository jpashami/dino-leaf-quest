# 🦕 Pip and the Magic Leaves

## Chapter 1 — A Tiny Egg
Deep in **Greenhollow Valley** stands the **Great Leaf Tree**, the oldest and kindest tree in the whole world.
Its glowing leaves keep the valley warm, green and full of flowers.

One stormy night, the grumpy **Night Wind** came whooshing through the valley.
*WHOOOOSH!* It blew the Great Tree's magic leaves all over the place —
under bushes, behind rocks, and far into the meadows.

The Great Tree grew sleepy and pale… and the flowers began to droop.

## Chapter 2 — Pip Hatches
At the roots of the Great Tree, a little egg went *crack… crack… POP!*
Out wobbled **Pip**, the tiniest dinosaur you ever saw.

The Great Tree whispered in a soft, leafy voice:

> "Little Pip, my magic leaves are scattered everywhere.
> Every leaf you eat will make you stronger.
> Grow big, grow brave — and bring the magic back to Greenhollow!"

Pip's tummy rumbled. *"Leaves? I LOVE leaves!"*

## Chapter 3 — Growing Stronger
With every leaf, Pip grows bigger and learns something new:

| Leaves | Pip becomes… | New power |
|---|---|---|
| 0  | **Tiny Hatchling** | Walking and munching |
| 5  | **Sniffy Pip** | A magic nose arrow that points to the nearest leaf |
| 12 | **Speedy Pip** | Runs super fast! |
| 20 | **Strong Pip** | Smashes big rocks to find leaves hidden underneath |
| 30 | **Roaring Pip** | A mighty ROAR that shakes leaves out of every bush nearby |
| 42 | **Pip the Leaf Guardian** 👑 | Glows with the full magic of the Great Tree |

**Hunting tips from the Great Tree:**
- 🌿 Green leaves = 1 point. ✨ Golden leaves = 3 points!
- Bushes that **twinkle** have a leaf hiding inside — walk into them!
- Rocks with a **green crack** have a leaf underneath — but only a Strong dino can smash them.

## Chapter 4 — The Valley Blooms
When Pip has eaten enough leaves, Pip becomes **the Leaf Guardian**.
Pip stands under the Great Tree and lets out the biggest, happiest roar ever:

**"ROOOOOAAAAAR!"**

The magic flows back into the Great Leaf Tree. It shines bright gold,
flowers pop open everywhere, and all the animals of Greenhollow cheer.

The Night Wind? It got so surprised that it turned into a gentle breeze —
and now it just tickles the flowers.

And Pip? Pip still loves eating leaves. Every. Single. Day. 🍃

**The End** (…or keep playing and eat ALL the leaves!)
